GUMZYY CALL FX v0.3.2 - FINAL DESIGN COMPILE FIX

1) Extract this ZIP into a new folder. Do not mix it with OFF SESSION TUNE or earlier GUMZYY folders.
2) Run Publish-To-GitHub.bat. The helper reuses sleeepboii123-bot/Gumzyy-Call-FX and pushes the updated project files to main.
3) Open the repository Actions tab and wait for the Windows x64 build and DSP tests.
4) Download GUMZYY-CALL-FX-v0.3.2-FINAL-Windows-x64-VST3 from the successful run.
5) Extract the artifact and double-click Install-GUMZYY-CALL-FX.bat. The installer requests administrator permission automatically.
6) Restart Ableton Live or rescan VST3 plug-ins.

FINAL DESIGN:
- The real JUCE window is rebuilt around the retro telephone handset, call display and GUMZYY artwork.
- Six large one-click character chains: ANGEL, PHONE, RADIO, WALKIE, MEGA and BROKEN.
- Four large modules: VOICE/FILTER, ANGEL/STEREO, COLOR/GRIT and SPACE/ECHO.
- Dedicated SHIMMER harmonic exciter, in addition to Input, Tone, Air, Width, Drive, Crush, Reverb and Delay.
- Big readable controls, numerical value boxes, live IN/OUT meters and a global wet/dry slider.
- A/B snapshots, DICE variation, RESET, BYPASS, BIG/SMALL, tooltips and double-click knob reset.
- Every visible knob controls a real DSP parameter and is host-automatable.

v0.3.2 BUILD FIXES:
- Renamed the banner asset to Assets/phone_banner.png so JUCE generates BinaryData::phone_banner_png and phone_banner_pngSize as referenced by the editor.
- Changed the title-area JUCE Rectangle to a mutable local before calling removeFromLeft().

LOW LATENCY:
The engine keeps a sample-by-sample, zero-lookahead design and declares 0 samples of plugin latency. Delay and reverb are intentional effects on the wet path; they do not delay the direct path. This is not a claim of zero total recording latency. CPU load, stability, sound quality and the JUCE editor still need Windows/Ableton verification.

The installer requests administrator permission before copying to the protected Common Files VST3 folder.
